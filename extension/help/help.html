Archangel Help

Getting Started

Open your AE site page. Open your DSIS site page.

The sidebar will refelct options available for the page you are visiting in AE. As you change from page to page in AE, it will update.

Auto Scouting

Go to any galaxy you have some scouts in. Click Disperse Now. Archangel will disperse scouts ships from the fleet with the most scouts in it, up to the Max Scouts setting (10 by default).

**Use the Options link to adjust your Max Scouts setting.

** If you refresh the page the scout is running in, it will stop the scout. Just click Continue to restart.
